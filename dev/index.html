Welcome to CARE!

Welcome to the CARE project – a Collaborative AI-Assisted Research Environment for reading, annotating, writing, and assessment workflows.

This documentation covers many examples to get you started really quick – both as a developer contributing to the extension of CARE or as a researcher using it for your research.

The project originated from the Intertext Initiative at the UKP Lab, TU Darmstadt, and is supported by the ATHENE Center, DFG, and the European Union.

For a live demo, visit https://demo.care.ukp.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de. Join our Discord to reach the team directly or discuss the project with the community.
